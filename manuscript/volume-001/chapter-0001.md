# Scene scene-clock

Mira found the harbor clock stopped at 03:17, its brass hands fused as if the storm had reached backward through the mechanism.

# Scene scene-lighthouse

The old lighthouse smelled of salt and dust. Beneath the lantern, Mira found a soot mark shaped like a date that had not happened yet.
